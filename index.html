Message from the Executive Director

Welcome to the official website of the Financial Intelligence Authority.

As we progress through 2026, I am pleased to share the important strides the Authority continues to make in strengthening Uganda’s financial integrity, protecting the financial system from abuse, and contributing to the safety and security of the broader economy.

The Financial Intelligence Authority’s mandate is anchored in the national goal of maintaining an effective framework to combat financial crime

About Us

The Financial Intelligence Authority(FIA) is Uganda’s national centre for the receipt of financial data, analysis and dissemination of financial intelligence to competent authorities.
